## 01 / ABOUT

I’m **Talha Raziq**, a Biotechnology graduate from **Gomal University**, Dera Ismail Khan, Pakistan. I’m building toward computational approaches to biological questions, bringing together biological systems, data analysis and code.

My interests sit at the intersection of **bioinformatics, computational biology, genomics and artificial intelligence / machine learning**. I enjoy turning biological data into interpretable results through research-oriented projects and reproducible computational workflows.

> *Biology is the language. Data is the lens.*
